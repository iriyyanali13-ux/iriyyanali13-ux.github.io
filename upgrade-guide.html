📈 PC Upgrade Path Guide

Started small? Here's exactly what to upgrade next — and in what order

🔑 The Golden Rule of PC Upgrades Always upgrade your GPU first for gaming — it gives the biggest FPS boost per rupee spent. Then RAM, then CPU. Never upgrade your CPU before your GPU unless your CPU is literally below minimum specs for the game.

⚙️ Upgrade Priority Order (for Gaming)

  • 1 GPU — Biggest FPS gains. Always upgrade this first.
  • 2 RAM — Going from 8GB → 16GB makes a noticeable difference in modern games.
  • 3 Storage — SSD massively reduces load times even if FPS stays the same.
  • 4 CPU — Only upgrade if your GPU is already strong and you're CPU bottlenecked.
  • 5 Motherboard — Only when switching CPU platforms (e.g. LGA1150 → AM4).
