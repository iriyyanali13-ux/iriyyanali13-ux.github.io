❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before building your budget PC in Pakistan

🛒 Buying Used Parts
Yes — RAM is actually one of the safest used parts to buy. It either works or it doesn't, and it rarely degrades over time. Run MemTest86 for one pass after buying. If it passes, you're good. Just make sure the speed matches your motherboard (DDR3 vs DDR4).
Used GPUs are fine if you test before buying. Always run FurMark for 15 minutes — temperatures should stay under 85°C and it shouldn't crash. Be extra careful with RX 580s and RX 570s as many were used for crypto mining. Avoid GPUs with bent pins, damaged connectors, or missing thermal pads.
No — never buy a used PSU. A failing power supply can fry every other component in your PC. Always buy your PSU new, even if it means cutting budget elsewhere. A basic 400W 80+ Bronze from Daraz or CZone costs around PKR 3,500–5,000 and is worth every rupee.
Download CrystalDiskInfo (free) and run it on the drive. It should show "Good" status and ideally less than 10,000 power-on hours. For SSDs, check the "Total Bytes Written" — anything under 100TB is fine for a budget buy. Avoid drives with "Caution" or "Bad" status.
⚡ Power Supply Questions
You need at least a 500W 80+ Bronze PSU for the RX 580. The card draws up to 185W under load, and combined with your CPU and other components you need headroom. Don't go below 500W and avoid no-brand cheap PSUs — they often deliver far less than rated.
A 400W–450W 80+ Bronze PSU is enough for the GTX 1650 Super. It's a very power-efficient card and pairs well with Ryzen CPUs. A 400W Seasonic or Gigabyte PSU from CZone is a safe choice.
🔧 Upgrades & Compatibility
Yes — but it depends on your platform. Ryzen builds (A320M, B350M) are much easier to upgrade since AM4 CPUs like Ryzen 5 3600 or even Ryzen 7 5700X can drop in with a BIOS update. Intel LGA1150 builds (i5-4570 etc.) are more limited. If upgrade potential matters, go Ryzen from the start.

🎮 Gaming Performance
Yes — Valorant is very well optimized. On a 20k build (i5-2400 + Intel HD) you can expect around 45+ FPS on low settings at 1080p. It's playable but not smooth for competitive play. For better Valorant performance, the 30k build gets you 90+ FPS which is a much better experience.
PUBG is one of the most demanding games on this list. You'll need at least the 40k build (RX 460) to get a playable 60 FPS on low settings. The 20k and 30k builds will struggle below 40 FPS. If PUBG is your main game, aim for at least the 50k build for a smooth experience.
